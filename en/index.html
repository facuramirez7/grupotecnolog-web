GEA decanters and centrifuges

The GEA decanter: from must to wine in a single machine

The decanter is the most versatile machine in the winery: it clarifies must, recovers wine from lees and sediment, and handles free-run must, press must and olive oil. As official GEA distributors we sell it, rent it for the harvest and commission it at your winery, with our own spare parts and after-sales service.

  • Must clarification
  • Wine recovery from lees
  • Free-run and press must
  • Olive oil
  • Harvest rental

In-house metal workshop

Custom stainless steel solutions for wineries and industry

We design and fabricate in stainless steel whatever each stage of the process needs: sanitary piping for wine transfer, heating and cooling heat exchangers, walkways and structures. We also do heat fusion and PVC chilled-water piping, with installations built to last and to perform at the peak of the harvest.
